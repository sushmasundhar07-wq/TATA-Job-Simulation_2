# GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation

## Overview

This repository documents my completion of the **GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation** offered through Forage in collaboration with Tata.

The simulation provided practical exposure to data analytics, risk profiling, AI-powered delinquency prediction, business reporting, and the design of responsible AI-driven collections strategies.

## Objectives

- Explore data to identify customer risk patterns and trends.
- Understand how AI can support delinquency prediction.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
- Communicate insights through stakeholder-focused reports.
- Develop a conceptual framework for an AI-driven collections strategy.

## Tasks Completed

### Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis and Risk Profiling
Explored data to understand customer characteristics, identify risk patterns, and support the assessment of delinquency risk.

### Task 2: Predicting Delinquency with AI
Worked on a structured predictive modelling approach to understand how AI can help identify customers who may be at risk of delinquency.

### Task 3: Business Reporting and Data Storytelling
Translated predictive insights into a clear business report, connecting analytical findings with recommendations for the collections team.

### Task 4: Implementing an AI-Driven Collections Strategy
Developed a high-level concept for an AI-powered collections system, focusing on applying insights at scale while considering responsible AI practices.
